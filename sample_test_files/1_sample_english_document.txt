LinguaFusion Offline Assistant
LinguaFusion is a private, local-first artificial intelligence assistant that runs 100% on your own hardware.
Features:
- Whisper Speech-to-Text with live correction
- NLLB-200 Neural Machine Translation
- RapidOCR text extraction from photos and documents
- Offline TTS Audio Reader
All computations stay strictly on your local graphics card with zero cloud servers involved.
